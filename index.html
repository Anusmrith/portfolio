KSEB Slab Watchdog — Kerala Electricity Tracker & Forecaster

Energy Analytics & PWA

"Kerala electricity meter tracker, daily burn rate predictor, 500-unit slab cliff radar, and household appliance simulator."

A specialized bilingual Progressive Web App (PWA) engineered to prevent unexpected electricity bill shock for Kerala households under the KSERC LT-1A tariff. Accurately simulates bi-monthly telescopic slabs and warns against the severe ₹1,420+ non-telescopic penalty cliff at 500 units before bills are issued.

  • Engineered the official KSERC LT-1A bi-monthly tariff engine modeling telescopic tiers (Slabs 1–5), fixed charges, meter rent, fuel surcharges, and the steep 500+ unit flat-rate cliff transition.

  • Built an interactive Kerala Household Appliance Inspector & Simulator modeling real-time energy draw for ACs, water pumps, and refrigeration with live duty-cycle sliders and savings calculators.
  • Implemented full Malayalam & English localization, procedural audio feedback using the Web Audio API (zero external audio assets), and one-click WhatsApp family alert summaries ("Stern Malayali Dad" mode).
  • Architected with Vite 6 and Vanilla ES6+ modules into an installable, offline-capable PWA with 100% passing automated mathematical test suites.

AttendWise — Cross-Platform Smart Attendance & ERP System

Full-Stack Mobile / PWA Full-Stack & Mobile Developer
Live Demo View Repository
"Cross-Platform Smart Attendance Tracker & ERP Integration System"

AttendWise is a cross-platform college attendance management and academic risk analyzer. Built with Vanilla JS, Supabase, and Capacitor, it features 1-click automated attendance synchronization with the EzyGo college portal (edu.ezygo.app), predictive recovery algorithms, and offline-first PWA & native Android/iOS capabilities.

  • Automated ERP Sync: Integrated authenticated session syncing with the EzyGo portal (edu.ezygo.app) to parse student reports and auto-populate attendance records with zero manual input.
  • Predictive Academic Calculators: Formulated dynamic algorithms that calculate exact consecutive classes needed to recover from low attendance or safe bunk margins before dipping below policy thresholds (e.g., 75%).
  • Cross-Platform & Offline-Ready: Deployed as an installable PWA with a network-first caching Service Worker, and built for native Android (.apk/.aab) and iOS via Capacitor 8.
  • Secure Cloud Backend: Architected user authentication and isolated multi-tenant data storage using Supabase and PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS).
  • Responsive UI/UX: Designed a mobile-first interface featuring notch/safe-area insets, interactive status pills (Safe/Warning/At Risk), and real-time aggregate statistics.
